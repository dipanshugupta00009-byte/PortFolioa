About Me

Who I am, what I study, and what I build outside the classroom.

I am Dipanshu Gupta, a second-year B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at IIMT College of Engineering. My academic focus complements my strong interest in software development and technology-driven problem-solving.

Beyond coursework, I actively build independent projects that reflect my technical curiosity. I am currently developing Jansatu, a civic-tech platform for Jharkhand designed to improve citizen engagement with local governance, which I have deployed live and continue to refine — including work on secure, Gmail-based user authentication.

I am also designing an original 3D multiplayer game featuring a detailed in-game economy, crafting system, and anime-inspired art direction, which reflects my interest in systems design and user experience beyond conventional coursework.

I aim to bring this same initiative to my college community — encouraging peers to participate in hackathons, competitions, and skill-building opportunities, while representing the platform with enthusiasm and professionalism.
